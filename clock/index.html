MMM Clock — Site Attendance

A low-cost clock-in system your workers use from their own cell phone. Every clock captures the time, GPS location, and whether the worker is inside a site geofence — then lands as a new row in a Google Sheet in real time. No app-store install, no monthly fee, works offline.

How it works
